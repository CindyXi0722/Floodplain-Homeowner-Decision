# How Homeowner's Decision Impact Disaster Recovery

An interactive widget that compares two ways a homeowner in a floodplain can protect themselves: **buying flood insurance** or **raising the house**. Sliders control the insurance premium, the cost of raising the house, the yearly chance of a flood, and how deep the water gets. A live graph and table show the total expected cost of each choice over 10 years.

**Live version:** https://cindyxigroup.github.io/flood-decision-explorer/

This project illustrates course concepts of insurance and disaster recovery: insurance reduces financial loss and helps a family recover after a flood, while raising the house prevents much of the damage in the first place.

Default values and slider ranges come from FEMA's National Flood Insurance Program (Risk Rating 2.0), published home-elevation cost estimates, and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers depth-damage curves. Sources are listed on the page.
